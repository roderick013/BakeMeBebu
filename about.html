Our Heartfelt Journey

Where warmth and kindness bake together.

Welcome to the home of Bake Me, Bebu! (B.M.B.). We started with a genuine love for baking comforting homemade treats and sharing that sweetness with people around us.

Based in Biñan, Laguna, we bake every cookie, banana loaf, cupcake, and mini bouquet cake with heart. To us, every customer is a Sweetheart, and every bake is a way to make someone's ordinary day a little warmer.
